# Feature ideas (not yet in the roadmap)

Candidate features to accept or reject. None of these are in `ROADMAP.md` yet.
Reply with the numbers you like (e.g. "keep 1, 4, 12–15") and they'll move
into the roadmap; the rest can be dropped or kept here for later.

Tier hint: **F** = would fit Free · **P** = would fit Pro.

## A. Smart planning
1. **Auto-scheduler** — splits a big task into daily chunks between now and the due date, based on your free time. **P**
2. **"Break it down"** — suggests subtasks from a task's title (e.g. "Research paper" → pick topic, find sources, outline, draft, edit). **P**
3. **Estimate learning** — suggests an estimate from how long similar tasks actually took you. **F**
4. **Exam study-plan generator** — enter an exam date and topics; get review sessions spread out before it. **P**
5. **Spaced-repetition reminders** — "review this topic again" nudges at growing intervals after studying it. **P**
6. **"I have 20 minutes"** — picks the best task that fits the time you have right now. **F**
7. **Procrastination radar** — flags tasks you keep snoozing or rescheduling. **F**
8. **Overload forecast** — "Thursday is packed — start the lab report on Monday." **F**
9. **Rubric to checklist** — paste a rubric, get a submission checklist. **P**
10. **Auto-detect subject** — guesses the subject from the task title. **F**
11. **Per-subject defaults** — e.g. Math homework always due 8:00 AM, 30 minutes. **F**
12. **Energy-based scheduling** — morning person or night owl; hard tasks go in your best hours. **P**
