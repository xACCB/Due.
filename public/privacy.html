Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information due. (the "app," hosted at dueplanner.vercel.app) collects, how it's used, and your choices. due. is a personal task/homework planner built by an independent developer.

Information we collect

If you use the app without signing in, your tasks and settings are stored only in your browser (localStorage) and are never sent to us.

If you choose to sign in with Google, we collect:

  • Your name, email address, and profile photo, provided by Google as part of sign-in.
  • The content you create in the app: tasks, due dates, subjects, notes, subtasks, your scratchpad text, and your display/theme preferences.

Signing in is optional and only used to sync your data across your own devices.

How we use your information

Your information is used solely to provide the app's functionality: authenticating you, and syncing your tasks and settings across the devices you sign in on. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party services we use

We rely on the following services to run the app. Each processes data on our behalf, under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Firestore database) — stores your account data and app content when you're signed in. See Firebase's privacy policy.
  • Google Sign-In — used to authenticate you. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise (via Firebase App Check) — helps verify that requests to our systems come from the real app rather than automated abuse. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics) — collects basic, aggregated usage data such as page views and how long the app is used, to help us understand which features are actually used. This does not include the content of your tasks or notes. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Vercel — hosts the app itself. See Vercel's privacy policy.

We do not use any advertising networks, and we do not sell or share your data for marketing purposes.

Data retention and deletion

If you sign in, your data is retained until you delete it or request deletion. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
